Skadden and S&C split Weil's restructuring bench, Sidley adds 35+ Weil lawyers

Thursday, November 19, 2026

Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom picked up Weil, Gotshal & Manges's global restructuring chair as part of a group of about 40 Weil lawyers. He will head Skadden's global restructuring group. Sullivan & Cromwell landed a 12-lawyer Weil restructuring team that includes the co-chair of Weil's US restructuring department. Sidley Austin brought on more than 35 Weil lawyers in New York, including 14 partners, led by the co-chair of Weil's corporate department and head of its M&A practice. Simpson Thacher & Bartlett added a five-partner litigation team from Weil in New York, Los Angeles and Boston. Kirkland & Ellis landed a Weil tax partner in Dallas. Dechert added two Weil lawyers as energy tax credit and finance partners in New York and DC. Jones Day poached a Latham & Watkins ITC patent litigation partner in DC. Cooley poached a Winston Taylor securities litigation partner in New York. Fisher Phillips added two privacy and cyber partners in New Jersey and Seattle.

On the business side, Sullivan & Cromwell named its next co-chairs, who take over on January 1, 2028. One of them will be the first woman to lead the firm. Bloomberg Law reports Kirkland & Ellis has done at least $11.8 million in free legal work under its deal with the Trump administration.
