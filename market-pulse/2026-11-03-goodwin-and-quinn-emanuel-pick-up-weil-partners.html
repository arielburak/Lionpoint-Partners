Goodwin and Quinn Emanuel pick up Weil partners

Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Goodwin Procter poached Weil, Gotshal & Manges' real estate co-head, who will join as a partner and co-leader of Goodwin's real estate industry group in New York. Bloomberg Law reports Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan is poaching a Weil, Gotshal & Manges trial partner in Los Angeles and New York who handles white collar and commercial litigation. Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan also added a New York appellate justice as a partner. Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP poached a Kirkland & Ellis LLP debt finance partner in New York. Troutman Pepper Locke poached a Kirkland & Ellis LLP investment funds partner in DC, who joins Troutman's private equity and investment funds practice as a partner. Kilpatrick poached a Kirkland & Ellis LLP class action litigation partner in Dallas. Sidley Austin LLP poached a Dechert LLP real estate finance partner in New York. In New York, Ropes & Gray added an asset management partner and Freshfields added a private funds and secondaries partner. Latham & Watkins added a tax controversy partner in DC. Akin added a corporate partner in Chicago. Morgan Lewis brought back an FDA and healthcare partner in DC. Arnall Golden Gregory LLP opened in Boston with a three-partner intellectual property team. It's only the firm's second new office in 77 years.

On the business side, a federal judge stripped Boies Schiller Flexner LLP of its interim class counsel role in the authors' AI training suit against Meta. Marvell Technology agreed to buy Lumentum in a deal that values Lumentum at $115 billion. Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati is advising Marvell. Davis Polk & Wardwell LLP is advising Lumentum.

Drawn from public reporting by Bloomberg Law, The American Lawyer, Law360, and New York Law Journal.
