Sidley keeps raiding Kirkland's funds bench

Thursday, November 5, 2026

Sidley Austin poached a Kirkland & Ellis investment funds partner in Los Angeles. Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe poached a Fenwick & West trademark partner in New York. Milbank signed a long-term lease on a new 48,000-square-foot office in Century City, which will house about 52 lawyers. Anderson Mori & Tomotsune, one of Japan's Big Four firms, plans to open offices in Dallas and Houston in spring 2027.

On the business side, the National Rifle Association is seeking arbitration against its former outside counsel, Brewer, Attorneys & Counselors, to recoup more than $200 million in legal fees. Brewer says it will defend its "record of excellence against these baseless claims." Cohen Seglias Pallas Greenhall & Furman sued the Republic of Sierra Leone in DC federal court, alleging the country owes $361,000 in unpaid legal bills. The Financial Times reports about 10 former Weil, Gotshal & Manges partners have hired a Foley & Lardner litigator to represent them in the firm's wind-down. The National Law Journal reports at least six of the 14 Big Law firms targeted by the Trump administration or that made deals with the president have responded to subpoenas in the American Bar Association's lawsuit over the administration's alleged law firm intimidation policy.
