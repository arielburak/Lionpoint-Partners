Skadden lands Weil's IP litigation leaders, Willkie adds a sports deals partner

Wednesday, November 18, 2026

Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom poached the two co-heads of Weil, Gotshal & Manges's IP, technology and science litigation practice, along with five more Weil IP litigation partners across California, Texas and DC. The co-heads join as co-chairs of Skadden's intellectual property litigation group, alongside a longtime Skadden partner. Massumi + Consoli poached a Weil executive compensation and benefits partner in New York. Milbank poached an A&O Shearman tax partner in New York. Willkie Farr & Gallagher poached the Sheppard Mullin partner who led the firm's sports industry team, who joins Willkie as a sports transactions partner in San Francisco. Willkie also brought a structured finance lawyer back from Soros Fund Management as a partner in New York. Sheppard added a finance partner and an energy and infrastructure partner in Chicago. Arnold & Porter added the former general counsel of the Office of the Director of National Intelligence as a national security partner in DC.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports a managing clerk who has worked at Moses & Singer since 1982 sued the firm, alleging age and race discrimination after his salary and hours were cut by 60% and his health insurance ended.
