Latham lands its Cahill trio, Skadden grabs Weil's litigation co-chair

Monday, November 16, 2026

Latham & Watkins poached three Cahill Gordon & Reindel finance partners in New York, including the leader of Cahill's digital infrastructure finance group, who join Latham's capital markets and public company representation practices. The American Lawyer reports Cahill has entered the merger market, according to a source familiar with the situation, and that web domains pairing Cahill with DLA Piper and with Willkie Farr & Gallagher have been registered in recent weeks. Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom poached three Weil, Gotshal & Manges partners in New York, including the co-chair of Weil's global litigation department, who also sat on Weil's management committee. They join Skadden in January. Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison poached a Goodwin Procter public M&A partner in New York, who co-led Goodwin's life sciences M&A group and joins Paul Weiss's M&A group. Greenberg Traurig added an Alston & Bird financial regulatory lawyer in DC, who joins as a shareholder in Greenberg Traurig's financial regulatory and compliance practice.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports a Richards, Layton & Finger partner was sanctioned Friday and ordered to pay $2,500 for submitting a brief that included AI hallucinations. Bloomberg Law, citing the Wall Street Journal, reports Baker McKenzie is shifting away from hourly billing as a default.

Sources

Drawn from public reporting by The American Lawyer and Bloomberg Law.
