Latham poised to grab three Cahill finance partners

Friday, November 13, 2026

Bloomberg Law reports Latham & Watkins is poised to pick up three Cahill Gordon & Reindel finance partners in the coming days. Two of them led Cahill's team on a $5.7 billion bond offering this spring to finance AI data center development in Indiana. Cahill's chair told Bloomberg Law that other firms have approached the firm about a potential combination. He says Cahill remains incredibly financially strong. He also says the firm is on track for double-digit revenue growth this year. He says the firm elected four new partners this week.

On the business side, the Financial Times reports Simpson Thacher & Bartlett will stop publicly reporting their financial results, effective immediately. Simpson Thacher says financial metrics provide "only a limited view of a firm." Bloomberg Law reports federal prosecutors charged a longtime Cravath, Swaine & Moore employee with insider trading, accusing him of trading ahead of deals tied to the firm's clients. Prosecutors say he made more than $70,000 on the trades between April 2025 and October 2026.
