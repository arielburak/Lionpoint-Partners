Latham lands a five-partner energy haul

Monday, November 2, 2026

Latham & Watkins poached three Baker Botts L.L.P. energy projects partners, including Baker Botts' global projects chair, as Latham builds a Houston base for its energy projects business. Two more energy lawyers came over from Sidley Austin LLP and Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP, both joining Latham as partners. White & Case poached three Cahill Gordon & Reindel LLP leveraged finance partners in New York. Sullivan & Cromwell LLP poached a Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton LLP antitrust partner in DC, who joins as co-head of S&C's antitrust group. Snell & Wilmer added two water law partners in Phoenix. Bloomberg Law reports the Weil, Gotshal & Manges LLP private equity partner once tapped to lead the firm is in advanced talks to join Kirkland & Ellis LLP, bringing 40 or more Weil professionals with her. Bloomberg Law also reports Crosby, a two-year-old law firm built around artificial intelligence, wants to buy pieces of Weil as the firm prepares to shut down.

On the business side, Kennedys is combining with Cunningham Swaim, a 14-lawyer aviation trial boutique, and opening a Dallas office.
