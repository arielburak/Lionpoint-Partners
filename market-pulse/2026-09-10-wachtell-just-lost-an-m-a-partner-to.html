Wachtell just lost an M&A partner to Linklaters

Thursday, September 10, 2026

Linklaters poached a Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz M&A partner in New York, who joins as managing partner of the Americas and co-chair of global M&A. He spent 30 years at Wachtell and starts in November. Freshfields poached a Weil, Gotshal & Manges white collar partner in New York, who joins as co-head of their global investigations practice. Winston Taylor added a Kirkland & Ellis investment funds partner in New York, plus a financial regulation and digital assets partner in New York the day before. Reed Smith brought in a private funds lawyer from Cravath, Swaine & Moore in New York, who joins as a partner in their global corporate group. DLA Piper added an investment funds partner in New York and a venture capital partner in San Francisco who joins from an in-house role. Foley & Lardner poached a Gibson, Dunn & Crutcher private equity partner in Houston. WilmerHale added a tax partner in New York. Willkie Farr & Gallagher brought back a litigator in DC, who rejoins as a partner after a clerkship at the Supreme Court.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports the country's largest firms hired fewer law school graduates last year for the first time since 2011.

Sources

Drawn from public reporting by Law.com, ALM, and Bloomberg Law.
