Simpson Thacher keeps building in Chicago

Wednesday, October 28, 2026

Simpson Thacher & Bartlett LLP poached two White & Case LLP M&A partners in Chicago. Simpson Thacher is also adding two Kirkland & Ellis partners there, in restructuring and executive compensation. Simpson Thacher says their Chicago team is growing to nearly a dozen partners. Ashurst Perkins Coie poached a Freshfields congressional investigations partner in DC. Mayer Brown added a derivatives and commodities partner in DC. Vinson & Elkins added a corporate partner in New York. Phillips Lytle LLP opened their first Southeast office, in Hilton Head, South Carolina, by combining with corporate boutique TaberPatrick.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports the head of Weil, Gotshal & Manges LLP's capital markets group told his junior lawyers to look for employment. Bloomberg Law also reports McDermott Will & Schulte promoted 36 lawyers to partner, down from 74 last year.
