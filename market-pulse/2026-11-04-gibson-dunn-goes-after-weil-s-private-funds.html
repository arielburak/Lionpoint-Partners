Gibson Dunn goes after Weil's private funds bench

Wednesday, November 4, 2026

Law.com reports Gibson, Dunn & Crutcher is hiring more than 80 private funds lawyers from Weil, Gotshal & Manges in the US, including around 12 partners. Dentons poached three Faegre Drinker Biddle & Reath insurance litigation partners from Philadelphia for their Short Hills, New Jersey office. Barnes & Thornburg brought in three Ballard Spahr real estate finance lawyers as partners in DC and Minneapolis. Meister Seelig & Schuster added 12 attorneys from New Jersey's Avelino Law for trusts, estates, tax and private client wealth planning. Ogletree Deakins added two employment litigation shareholders in Los Angeles and an employment shareholder in San Diego. Ogletree Deakins also brought in an Amazon employment lawyer as a shareholder in Seattle.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports the Democrats' midterm wins give them expanded power to investigate the deals major law firms cut with the White House.
