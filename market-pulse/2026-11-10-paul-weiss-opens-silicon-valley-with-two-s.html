Paul Weiss opens Silicon Valley with two S&C tech partners

Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison poached two Sullivan & Cromwell technology partners in Silicon Valley, who join as the first partners in the firm's new Silicon Valley office. The pair advised Broadcom on its $63 billion bank financing to buy chips for Anthropic. Clifford Chance poached a Sidley Austin sports private equity partner for its US private equity practice, who will also chair the firm's global entertainment and sports sectors team. Willkie Farr & Gallagher is adding three Hogan Lovells Cadwalader private equity and funds partners in DC, part of a five-partner asset management team focused on secondaries. Greenberg Traurig added a franchise and distribution partner in DC, and Simpson Thacher & Bartlett added an energy and infrastructure partner in DC. Polsinelli added a trademark litigator in New York who joins as a shareholder. Alston & Bird added two real estate partners in Southern California, one in Century City and one in Los Angeles.

On the business side, a federal judge in Chicago let JM Eagle pursue racketeering claims against plaintiffs' firms Simmons Hanly Conroy and Sokolove Law, which the pipe maker accuses of using fraudulent tactics in asbestos litigation.
