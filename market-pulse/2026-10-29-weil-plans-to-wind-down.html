Weil plans to wind down

Thursday, October 29, 2026

Bloomberg Law reports Weil, Gotshal & Manges LLP plans to wind down. Weil says its management committee recommended an orderly wind-down. Partners are expected to vote in the coming days. Bloomberg Law also reports associates and staff were told they'll get 90 days of salary and benefits. Morrison Foerster poached two Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP partners in New York. One will lead Morrison Foerster's business litigation and disputes practice. Honigman LLP opened a Boston office, adding three Womble Bond Dickinson patent lawyers as partners.

On the business side, Latham & Watkins advised DoorDash on its $17 billion plan to buy Instacart. Cooley LLP and Cravath, Swaine & Moore LLP advised Instacart.
