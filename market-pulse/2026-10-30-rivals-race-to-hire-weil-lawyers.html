Rivals race to hire Weil lawyers

Friday, October 30, 2026

Bloomberg Law reports rivals are racing to hire Weil, Gotshal & Manges LLP lawyers. Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP hired a number of Weil junior lawyers this week, Sullivan & Cromwell LLP partners are talking to potential hires, and Simpson Thacher & Bartlett LLP is trying to add more partners after bringing on over a dozen people from Weil in recent weeks. The American Lawyer reports one Weil candidate fielded 25 solicitations from firms on Thursday alone, according to a recruiter. Bloomberg Law also reports Weil aims to wind down without a bankruptcy, following the path Stroock & Stroock & Lavan took. That hinges on reaching a payment plan with creditors.

On the business side, Kirkland & Ellis LLP and Cravath, Swaine & Moore LLP guided Thrive Holdings' $7.6 billion all-cash deal to buy ADT. Fox Rothschild LLP is out from under a proposed class action over its May data breach, after the plaintiffs voluntarily dismissed it Wednesday in federal court in Pennsylvania.
