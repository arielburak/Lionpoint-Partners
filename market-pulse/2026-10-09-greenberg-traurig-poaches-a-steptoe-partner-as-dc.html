Greenberg Traurig poaches a Steptoe partner as DC hiring stays busy

Friday, October 9, 2026

Greenberg Traurig, LLP poached a Steptoe LLP antitrust and life sciences litigation partner in Washington, DC, who joins as a shareholder in Greenberg Traurig's litigation practice. He had joined Steptoe as a partner in February. Foley & Lardner LLP added an assistant chief from the Justice Department's Antitrust Division in DC, who joins as a partner in Foley's antitrust and competition practice. Arnall Golden Gregory LLP added a former CMS litigator in DC, who joins as a partner in AGG's healthcare practice. Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck is opening an Atlanta office with two government relations veterans from Atlanta-based MCO Group. They start November 2.
