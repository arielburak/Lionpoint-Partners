Kirkland lands a 40-lawyer Weil deals team

Friday, November 6, 2026

Kirkland & Ellis announced a 40-lawyer deals team from Weil, Gotshal & Manges is joining the firm. The American Lawyer reports a Weil, Gotshal & Manges private equity partner in Dallas is moving to Simpson Thacher & Bartlett. Bloomberg Law reports Weil has told its remaining equity partners they will be considered withdrawn by year-end. Bloomberg says that frees them to find new jobs and keep their share of profit. White & Case poached a Goodwin Procter M&A partner in New York. Wilmer Cutler Pickering Hale and Dorr hired the Treasury Department's former under secretary for domestic finance as a partner in DC. Dechert added a corporate and securities partner in Dallas. Maynard Nexsen added a nine-lawyer labor and employment team from Constangy, Brooks, Smith & Prophete in New York, New Jersey and St. Louis. Cravath, Swaine & Moore is promoting 11 lawyers to partner, its largest class since 2020.

On the business side, a New York judge sanctioned boutique Bochner Law for what he called a "frivolous" malpractice suit against Thompson Hine. Cravath, Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom and Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison guided Liberty Media's purchase of founder John Malone's super-voting shares.

Sources

Drawn from public reporting by Bloomberg Law, The American Lawyer, New York Law Journal, and The National Law Journal.
