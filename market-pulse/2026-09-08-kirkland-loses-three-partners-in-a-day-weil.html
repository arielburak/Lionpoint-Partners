Daily Market Pulse

Kirkland loses three partners in a day. Weil takes back a Freshfields private credit co-head.

Tuesday, September 8, 2026

Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison poached a Kirkland & Ellis investment funds partner in New York, Sidley Austin poached a Kirkland & Ellis tax partner in Century City, and Cooley poached a Kirkland & Ellis fund formation partner in Chicago. Weil, Gotshal & Manges brought back Freshfields' global co-head of private credit and capital solutions as a banking and finance partner in New York. Paul Hastings poached a Cooley capital markets partner in New York and Silicon Valley. Katten added a privacy and AI partner in Los Angeles who chaired Kasowitz Benson Torres' data strategy, privacy and security team. Shipman & Goodwin opened their first Texas office in Dallas with two restructuring shareholders from Ferguson Braswell Fraser Kubasta. Sullivan & Cromwell brought in Fox's former chief legal and policy officer as a partner in Los Angeles. Venable brought in a former SEC commissioner as a partner in DC for their securities and regulatory practices. Bloomberg Law reports a Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz restructuring lawyer is leaving after more than 30 years to become a senior adviser at M3 Partners.

On the business side, Bloomberg Law reports the top 50 firms hired 1,086 lateral partners through August, up from 901 a year earlier, citing SurePoint Legal Insights. Payouts from L.A. County's $4 billion sex abuse settlement have been delayed while two plaintiffs' firms face probes, the Los Angeles Times reports.

Sources

Drawn from public reporting by The American Lawyer, The Recorder, and Bloomberg Law.
